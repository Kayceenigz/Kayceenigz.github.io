About

I'm a game developer and designer who likes games as whole systems: how they play, how they balance, and how they earn.

Unity is my main engine, with Unreal (Blueprints and C++) and Roblox Studio alongside it. I work at Juego Studios, and earlier I interned at Kyodai Gameworks on system design, balancing, monetization models and player metrics.

This site is hand-built too. Outside of work I ride bikes and chase anything with an engine.
